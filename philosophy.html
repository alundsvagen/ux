Philosophy

Branding shouldn’t be a luxury for small, sustainable shops.

We believe branding is not decoration. It’s clarity, confidence, and consistency — so your small, sustainable e-commerce brand can show up like it belongs in the big leagues, without you becoming a designer.

Who this is for

Small e-commerce founders who care about sustainability and:

  • Don’t have an in-house designer or agency.
  • Feel their visuals are “all over the place”.
  • Want to look trustworthy without spending months on a rebrand.

What you want

  • A brand that feels clear, cohesive, and “you”.
  • Simple tools so anyone on the team can stay on-brand.
  • More time to build the business, less time fiddling with layouts.
01 · The reality

Branding feels important, but also exhausting.

Most sustainable e-commerce founders aren’t trying to become art directors. You’re trying to ship orders, answer customer emails, and keep things moving — while knowing your brand doesn’t quite match the quality of what you sell.

You probably recognise some of this:

  • Your logo, packaging, website, and socials all look slightly different.
  • You’re piecing things together from templates, freebies, and “what looks okay”.
  • You’re not sure if your brand feels premium, or just “home-made”.
  • You spend more time tweaking designs than you want to admit.

Underneath that is a simple tension

  • You care deeply about your product and values.
  • You know first impressions matter in e-commerce.
  • You don’t want branding to become a second job.
02 · What you actually want

You don’t want “a logo”. You want ease, trust and recognition.

When people say they “need branding”, they’re rarely asking for more design files. They’re asking for a calmer, more confident way of showing up in the world.

Outcomes you’re chasing

  • Customers recognise you instantly across channels.
  • Your shop feels trustworthy enough to try — and to buy from again.
  • You can charge fairly for the care and quality you put in.

And how you want it to feel

  • No more guessing which font or colour to use.
  • Less starting from a blank canvas, more “drop it into the system”.
  • Confidence that your brand looks put-together, even on busy days.
03 · Our philosophy

Branding is how you show up, again and again, without burning out.

We design brand systems for small sustainable e-commerce teams who want to look and feel like “a real brand” — without the drama of a big agency or the pressure of doing it all alone.

Principle 01

You shouldn’t have to be a designer to look professional.

Your energy belongs in your product, your customers, and your operations. Our job is to give you a visual system that feels intuitive and forgiving, so non-designers can still create on-brand things.

Principle 02

Consistency builds trust faster than perfection.

We’d rather your brand be 90% consistent everywhere than 110% perfect in one place. Simple, repeatable rules make it easier to show up the same way on your site, packaging, email, and socials.

Principle 03

Sustainability and success belong together.

We design identities that feel calm, honest, and solid — serious enough for growth, soft enough to reflect the care and responsibility behind your products.

04 · How our systems work for you

From “I’m making it up” to “we have a brand system”.

We don’t just hand you a logo. We give you a small, well-designed set of rules, assets, and templates so anyone on your team can stay on-brand in a few clicks.

In practice, this usually looks like:

  • 01 · Clarify — we define your core look: colour, type, tone, and how you want to feel in the market.
  • 02 · Systemise — we turn that into a compact brand system: styles, layouts, and ready-to-use components.
  • 03 · Hand over — you get a “brand shelf” you can grab from: social templates, packaging direction, web basics.

What you walk away with

  • A calm, cohesive visual language you recognise as “you”.
  • Simple guidelines that a non-designer can actually follow.
  • Less time designing, more time running the business.

For small, sustainable & values-driven e-commerce brands.
